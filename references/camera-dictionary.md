# Camera & motion dictionary

Vocabulary for SHOT lines. Camera moves, focus effects and speed effects are written with their slash prefix (`/push in`) by default, or as plain words when the user's app is known not to read slash commands; framing, angle and lens terms are written as plain words in the framing slot (`medium close-up, low angle`). Combine up to two slash tokens per shot (`/crane up + /tracking shot`). Transitions go in edit notes unless they happen inside a clip. The † mark is only provenance and is never written into prompts.

## Camera movements
- /orbit 360 †: Smoothly circles subject.
- /arc shot: Partial curved move around the subject (90–180°).
- /drone fly †: Aerial dynamic movement.
- /fpv drone: Fast, agile first-person drone flight through spaces.
- /push in †: Slowly moves toward subject.
- /pull out †: Moves backward from scene.
- /dolly in / out †: Cinematic controlled movement.
- /dolly zoom: Dolly and zoom in opposite directions; background warps while subject stays the same size.
- /tracking shot †: Follows moving subject.
- /leading shot: Camera moves backward in front of a walking subject, facing them.
- /side tracking †: Moves alongside subject.
- /truck left / right: Camera slides sideways, parallel to the scene.
- /crane up †: Rises vertically for reveal.
- /crane down: Descends vertically into the scene.
- /pedestal up / down: Camera body rises or lowers without tilting.
- /pan left / right: Camera rotates horizontally from a fixed point.
- /tilt up / down: Camera rotates vertically from a fixed point.
- /zoom in / out: Lens zoom with no camera movement.
- /crash zoom: Very fast zoom in for sudden emphasis.
- /reveal: Camera moves past a foreground object to uncover the subject.
- /static: Locked-off camera, no movement.
- /roll: Camera rotates on its lens axis.
- /spiral: Camera rotates while moving toward or away from the subject.

## Framing & angle
- extreme wide: Subject tiny within a vast environment.
- wide shot: Full environment with subject readable.
- establishing shot: Opening wide that sets the location.
- full shot: Subject head to toe.
- medium shot: Subject from the waist up.
- medium close up: Subject from the chest up.
- close up †: Attention tight on subject.
- extreme close up: Single detail (eyes, mouth, object).
- insert: Cutaway to an object or detail.
- two-shot: Two subjects in frame together.
- over the shoulder: Framed from behind one subject toward another or toward the scene.
- pov: Seen through the subject's eyes.
- eye level: Neutral camera height.
- low angle †: Positions below the subject.
- worm's eye: Extreme low angle from ground level.
- high angle: Positions above the subject, looking down.
- bird's eye: Extreme high angle, far above.
- top down †: Direct overhead perspective.
- dutch angle: Tilted horizon for unease.

## Focus & lens
- /rack focus †: Transitions focus depth.
- /shallow depth †: Strong background blur.
- /deep focus: Foreground and background both sharp.
- /macro shot †: Extreme close-up textures.
- /wide angle lens: Exaggerated depth and perspective.
- /telephoto: Compressed depth, flattened background.
- /fisheye: Extreme curved wide-angle distortion.
- /tilt-shift: Selective focus band, miniature look.
- /split diopter: Two planes in focus at once.
- /anamorphic: Wide cinematic frame with oval bokeh and horizontal flares.
- /lens flare: Light source streaking across the lens.

## Speed, motion & effects
- /speed ramp †: Smooth slow-to-fast transition.
- /slow motion: Action slowed for weight or detail.
- /fast motion: Action sped up.
- /time-lapse: Long passage of time compressed.
- /hyperlapse: Time-lapse with the camera travelling.
- /freeze frame: Motion stops on a single frame.
- /bullet time: Action frozen or slowed while the camera moves around it.
- /whip pan †: Rapid pan with motion blur.
- /handheld †: Adds subtle instability.
- /shaky cam: Strong handheld shake for chaos or impact.
- /steady cam †: Smooth stabilized movement.
- /gimbal: Smooth floating stabilized movement.
- /hero shot †: Frames as a cinematic moment.
- /parallax †: Layered depth between foreground and background.

## Transitions
- /hard cut: Instant cut to the next shot.
- /match cut: Cut between two shots matched by shape or motion.
- /smash cut: Abrupt jarring cut, often loud to silent.
- /cross dissolve: One shot fades into the next.
- /fade to black / fade in: Transition through black.
- /whip transition: Whip pan that blurs into the next shot.
- /flash cut: Very brief inserted shot (under 1 s).
