# Negative prompt library

Candidates for each clip's `[CLIP n]` items (the [BASE] list and the selection rules live in clip-template.md). The most valuable items are usually not here: the 2–3 specific ways this clip's key beat could go wrong. Use this library for the rest. Skip any item that would forbid something the unit intends to show.

## Face & identity
changing eye color, cross-eyed, extra teeth, changing age, changing skin tone, changing hair color, face melting, plastic skin, uncanny smile

## Body & anatomy
missing fingers, fused fingers, missing limbs, twisted joints, wrong proportions, floating body, sliding feet, feet through floor, broken neck angle

## Costume & props
outfit color shift, missing accessory, accessory on wrong side, prop changing shape, prop disappearing, prop duplicating, prop in wrong hand, mark or scar on wrong side

## Environment & architecture
extra or missing structures, bending walls, melting floor, wrong room layout, windows where there are none, door where there is none, objects popping in or out, background morphing

## Lighting & color
time of day change, wrong light color, overexposed, underexposed, blown highlights, color shift mid-clip, inconsistent shadows; "lighting changing between shots" only when every light in the clip is meant to be static

## Motion & camera
frame stutter, warping during motion, rubbery movement, unnatural slow motion, unwanted camera shake, sudden zoom, morphing transition, objects moving during a freeze

## Text & graphics
misspelled words, random letters, captions, UI overlay, signature, frame border

## Style & quality
cartoonish shift, photoreal shift, blurry, noise, compression artifacts, oversharpened, oversaturated, washed out, duplicated frames

## Audio
mumbled speech, lips not matching words, speech from a silent character, background music, music restarting at each cut, sudden volume jump, clipped audio, echo in a small space, missing ambience, robotic voice

## Tone
exaggerated expression, overacting, screaming, comedic tone, gore, blood
